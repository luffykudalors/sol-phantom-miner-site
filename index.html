Rewards should be transparent.

SOL Phantom Miner is an Android-focused project exploring how people can earn rewards through eligible tasks, with clear terms and a path toward Solana payouts.

Explore the project Development status
Important: SOL Phantom Miner does not mine Solana. The project is in development; real tasks, reward funding, and SOL withdrawals are not yet available. No earnings are promised.

Designed around verified rewards

Our goal is to build a straightforward rewards experience. Features will only be described as live once they are implemented and tested.

📋

Eligible tasks

Explore third-party tasks or offers only when a provider accepts the app and its audience. Provider availability may vary by country.

🛡️

Verified credits

Plan for server-verified completions, transparent reward records, and fraud checks rather than trusting balances stored only on a phone.

◎

Solana payouts

Future payouts would require actual funded rewards, a supported payout process, and transaction fees. This feature is not active yet.
